# Kredit foto bunga / Flower photo credits

The real flower and leaf photos in this folder come from **Wikimedia Commons**. They were cut out from their
backgrounds, colour-toned (softer, warmer pinks), resized and arranged together.

The finished images (`corner.webp`, `garland.webp`, `bouquet.webp`, `petals.webp`) are adaptations, so they are
shared under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), as the share-alike terms of the sources require.
